How it works

Recipes

Put a VM together from building blocks: a platform – NixOS or macOS –, modules (a desktop, a terminal, an editor, LaTeX …, written in Nix), a look and key bindings. Install a recipe and what it is made of comes along – or put your own together in the app. Browse the catalog.

Images

omaflake builds an immutable image from each recipe. Images never change.

Instances

Run disposable instances from the app – the menu bar on a Mac, a native window on Linux – or the omaf CLI. Reset throws an instance's state away and starts over from the image.

Install on a Mac

Until omaflake is signed by Apple's Developer ID, macOS blocks the app when it comes from a browser. Either way below works.

In the Terminal

Downloads, checks and unpacks the app into /Applications. Files fetched with curl are not blocked.

curl -fLO <download URL>
shasum -a 256 -c <file>.sha256
ditto -x -k <file> /Applications

From the browser

Unzip the download and move omaflake to Applications. Open it once – macOS refuses. Then System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to omaflake and confirm.

Install on Linux

On Linux omaflake runs NixOS guests. It comes as a Nix package with the app, a desktop entry and the omaf CLI, for x86_64 and aarch64 – on NixOS or any distribution with Nix.

With Nix

Installs omaflake and QEMU, then points omaflake at its image flake (the Linux package does not bring it yet).

nix profile install github:omaflake/omaflake#omaflake-gui nixpkgs#qemu
omaf config --flake 'github:omaflake/omaflake?dir=nix'
omaf doctor

Details – KVM access, other distributions, what works on a Linux desktop so far – in the Linux guide.

Requirements

  • Mac: Apple silicon and macOS 27 or later – NixOS and macOS guests
  • Linux: x86_64 or aarch64 with KVM, Nix with flakes (or NixOS) – NixOS guests
  • Nix for NixOS images – on a Mac with a Linux builder; the app's Doctor checks what your computer brings and, on a Mac, sets both up by click
  • Space: NixOS images take a few GB each; a macOS base downloads about 27 GB from Apple

What alpha means

  • Things will break. Your VMs, images and settings live in ~/.omaflake – the app can move that folder.
  • No automatic updates yet: the Doctor tells you when a new version is out, you install it the same way.
  • Signed and notarized builds follow once falkOSS, the non-profit behind omaflake, has its Apple developer account.
  • The omaf CLI comes with the app: on a Mac the Doctor offers to put it on your PATH; the Linux package installs it.
